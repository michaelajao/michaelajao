- 🔬 I'm a **Research Fellow** at the **Research Centre for Computational Science and Mathematical Modelling (CSM), Coventry University**. I work on **HOPE-MOVE**, an Innovate UK-funded project using AI and machine learning to personalise digital support for movement and healthy lifestyles.

- 🎓 I completed my **PhD in Computational Science & Mathematical Modelling**, focused on **spatiotemporal deep learning and optimisation for epidemic forecasting and healthcare resource allocation**.

- 🧠 My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning, scientific computing and public health**: classical and deep forecasting, graph neural networks, hybrid mechanistic–neural models, physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and time-series analysis.

- 👨‍🏫 I'm also an **Associate Lecturer at QA Higher Education (QAHE)**, teaching computing, client-side development and deep learning, and mentoring students on MSc projects and dissertations.

- 🎖️ I'm an **Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA)**.

- 👯 I'm open to collaboration on **scientific machine learning, time-series and graph forecasting, computer vision, PINNs, and applied AI for health**.

- 📫 Reach me at **ajaoolarinoyemichael@gmail.com**

- ⚡ Fun fact: **I believe my research can change the world—right after I find that missing semicolon.**

## 🔭 Research Focus

- **Forecasting for Public Health** — Building and benchmarking statistical, sequence deep-learning, graph-based spatiotemporal, and hybrid mechanistic–neural forecasting models.

- **Scientific Machine Learning** — Applying PINNs to cardiovascular haemodynamics, including pulsatile flow, wall-shear-stress prediction and arterial aneurysm modelling.

- **Digital Health & Clinical ML** — Developing machine-learning services and decision-support tools for the HOPE-MOVE digital health intervention.

- **Healthcare Resource Allocation** — Optimisation and modelling for distributing constrained healthcare resources during infectious-disease outbreaks.
